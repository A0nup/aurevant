The House Philosophy

“True luxury does not clamor for attention. It exists in the weight of the drape, the precision of the stitch, and the quiet confidence of the wearer.”

AUREVANT was established on a single principle: that modern elegance in India requires an international luxury standard stripped of transient trends and loud logos. We design for the connoisseur who appreciates the distinction between mere expense and genuine refinement.

Every collection is produced in disciplined batches. Fabrics are chosen solely for their structural integrity and natural sensory touch — extra-long staple Egyptian Giza cottons, unwashed Okayama shuttle-loom selvedge, and double-faced Mongolian cashmere.

The Discipline of Detail

Atelier Craftsmanship

The integrity of an AUREVANT garment is revealed through rigorous physical inspection.

01

Fiber Architecture

We reject synthetic compromises. Our cottons represent the top 1% harvest of Egyptian Giza 45 and Peruvian Pima. Double-mercerisation imparts a subtle liquid luster that deepens through years of wear rather than washing away.

Fiber Length: 36mm+ • Single-Origin Traceability
02

Structural Construction

Our tailored shirts feature hand-turned semi-spread Milanese collars with removable brass collar stays. Buttonholes are finished with 100% silk thread, securing genuine Australian mother-of-pearl buttons carved from sustainable Pinctada shells.

Single-Needle Felled Seams • Split Yoke
03

Longevity & Care

Luxury is measured by longevity. We engineer pre-shrunk ribbing and reinforced shoulder taping so your garment maintains its architectural contour wash after wash. Every piece is supplied with a cedar hanger and breathable garment envelope.
